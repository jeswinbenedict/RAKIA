## Beyond the hackathon

- Interoperability adapters for Open mHealth and HL7 FHIR Observation resources.
- Evaluation against public analog-mission datasets, subject to their licences.
- Retrieval-only explanations grounded in verified evidence (no generative diagnosis).
- Embedded build of `librakia` for habitat sensor hubs.
